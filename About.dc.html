About Hozeh — Marketing, Technology & Growth
HOZEH / ABOUT

We started Hozeh because we thought marketing could be done differently.

Hozeh was founded by Nirmal and Allan Gabriel — two young entrepreneurs who met through music and eventually decided to build something of their own. Today, Hozeh brings marketing, creative work, technology, AI, automation, and business growth together under one roof.

01 — ORIGIN

It started with a gig.

ON STAGE · BANGALORE

Nirmal was leading his band for a gig in Bangalore. The singer wasn't available.

A violinist introduced him to Allan.

Allan joined the band. The two became close, and while still in college they started thinking about building something of their own.

That introduction turned into a band. The band turned into a friendship. And the friendship turned into a company.

Music taught them something unexpectedly valuable: how to sell. Every gig meant communicating value, pitching their skills, and proving why someone should choose them.
